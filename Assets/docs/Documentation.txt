We are making a multiplayer elimination game inspired by "tag" but with bombs in Unity 6 using Fish-Net and UGS.
Give the roadmap of development

Game Mechanics:
- Players start in an arena with obstacles.
- One or more players starts with a bomb.
- The bomb has a countdown timer (e.g., 20 seconds).
- Players must pass the bomb by tagging another player before the timer reaches zero.
- When the bomb gets passed, the new holder becomes the tagger (holding the bomb).
- Tagger has the ability to Dash, and Runners have the ability to Dive for dodging the tagger.
- When the timer runs out, the player holding the bomb explodes (+1 Death).
- The last player who passed the bomb to the victim before explosion gets +1 Kill.
- Exploded player respawns after a 2-second delay at a random spawn position in the arena.
- A new bomb is immediately assigned randomly to a player.

Round & Match System:
- Single Round Timed Match (e.g., 3-minute match timer).
- Players continuously respawn upon explosion until match timer reaches zero.

Victory Condition:
- When the match timer ends, players are ranked by Score:
  Score = (Number of Kills - Number of Times Died).
- Player with the highest Score wins the match.

Optional Features: will be implements later when game core is ready
- Power-ups in the arena (speed boost, invisibility for 3s).
- Sticky bombs which when thrown on players (runner and taggers), they will get stuck to ground for a while. Everyone will have 2 sticky bombs in start and if eleminated their left over bombs can be picked by others
- Arena may shrink over time to increase tension.
- Scoreboard shows survival time, kills, and eliminations.
