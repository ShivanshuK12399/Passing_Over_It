 We are making a multiplayer elimination game inspired by "tag" but with bombs in unity6 using net code.
Give the roadmap of development

Game Mechanics:
- Players start in an arena with obstacles.
- One or more players start with a bomb.
- The bomb has a countdown timer (e.g., 20 seconds).
- Players must pass the bomb by tagging another player before the timer reaches zero.
- When bomb gets passed the new holder will become the tagger.
- When the timer runs out, the player holding the bomb is eliminated.
- Tagger has ability to dash and runner has ability to dive for dodging tagger.

Round System:
- The game is divided into 3 rounds.
- Each round continues until the player count reduces to a set threshold:
  - Round 1: Continue until X players remain.
  - Round 2: Continue until Y players remain.
  - Round 3: Final round, last player standing wins.

Additional Rules:
- Eliminated players do NOT respawn (they become spectators).
- At the start of each round, the bomb is randomly assigned to a surviving player.
- If multiple bombs are desired in later rounds, add them for faster eliminations.
- To avoid stalemates, if the timer is close to ending and players refuse to take the bomb, the current holder is forced to explode.
- When last round no sticky bombs will be allowed

Victory Condition:
- The last surviving player after Round 3 is the winner.

Optional Features: will be implements later when game core is ready
- Power-ups in the arena (speed boost, invisibility for 3s).
- Sticky bombs which when thrown on players (runner and taggers), they will get stuck to ground for a while. Everyone will have 2 sticky bombs in start and if eleminated their left over bombs can be picked by others
- Arena may shrink over time to increase tension.
- Scoreboard shows survival time, number of passes, and eliminations.
